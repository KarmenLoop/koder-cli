Publication is authorized for Windows and macOS. Native Windows beta testing is owned by the product team and does not block publication. This file distinguishes implementation checks from device/account acceptance; it does not assert that all platforms or external services were tested.

See [release notes](docs/RELEASE-0.4.17.md), [中文说明](docs/RELEASE-0.4.17.zh.md), and the [promise audit](docs/PROMISE-AUDIT-0.4.17.md). Local regression, packaged-byte, upgrade and production-download receipts are kept by the release task. The original failed audit remains retained.

Historical acceptance documents describe their original tested builds and are not evidence for 0.4.17 devices.

The complete prior status, including failures and holds, is preserved without edits in [the 0.4.16 historical record](docs/history/RELEASE-STATUS-0.4.16.md).
