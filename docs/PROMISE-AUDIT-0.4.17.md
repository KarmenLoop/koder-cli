## Reading the evidence

A fixture pass establishes the covered contract, not a paid-model score, native GUI result or external business receipt. Historical device records remain historical; the new artifact requires its own beta record. Unsupported or missing prerequisites are reported instead of simulated. The signed macOS locked-use component, verified automatic method adoption without a paired comparison, and guaranteed instantaneous mail visibility are not advertised as completed capabilities.

## Recovery and rollback

Retain projects, task receipts and history. A failed or UNKNOWN operation must be reconciled before retry; steering does not erase it. The prior immutable `cli-0.4.16` remains available. This release does not grant new computer permissions, change credentials or register services on a tester's machine without the existing user workflow.
