# Commercial licensing and private delivery

New proprietary distributions adopting Koder-EULA-1.1 are free for personal, noncommercial local use. Commercial use, paid client work, internal organizational work and organizational deployment require separate prior written authorization. The personal license does not permit secondary development or changes to the proprietary implementation. Supported configuration and independently authored extensions remain available within the applicable use scope.

The KARMEN LOOP team offers company-licensing and dedicated-private-repository inquiries through [info@karmenloop.com](mailto:info@karmenloop.com). Run koder business for a contact template. Include the organization, intended use, approximate user count, deployment requirements and private-repository needs; do not include credentials or confidential documents.

A separate written agreement defines users, deployment scope, repository contents, access, support, maintenance, pricing and any development rights. There is no implied source-code delivery, automatic purchase, instant authorization or provisioned repository. Repository access alone does not grant modification or development rights.

This policy applies prospectively to the distributions that adopt it. Previous distributions and third-party components retain their original licenses. The full EULA, license-scope notice and third-party notices control their respective components. User-provided reference collections are not relicensed as Koder property.

An optional [GitHub star](https://github.com/KarmenLoop/koder-cli) helps others find Koder. It does not unlock features, change a license, or affect installation. The installer displays a link without waiting for a response or opening a browser.
